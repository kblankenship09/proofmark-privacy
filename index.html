Privacy Policy

Proofmark Capture turns your clicks into a step-by-step guide, entirely on your own machine. This page is the complete, plain-language account of what that involves — there's no fine print elsewhere.

In short
  • Everything Proofmark records stays on your device — nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent to us, ever.
  • Recording only happens while you've pressed Start, and only screenshots of the window you click into — not your whole screen, and not your keystrokes.
  • By default only left-clicks and Enter/Tab are recorded; an off-by-default setting can also capture right-clicks, scrolls, and drags if you turn it on.
  • A best-effort filter blacks out likely-sensitive windows automatically, but it can miss things — review a guide before you share it.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third parties of any kind.
01

What's recorded

Proofmark only records anything while you've explicitly clicked Start Recording. While it's on, it watches for input system-wide — not just inside the Proofmark window:

  • Left-clicks — always recorded while a session is active.
  • Enter and Tab key presses — recorded only as the fact that Enter or Tab was pressed, never any other key.
  • Right-clicks, scroll gestures, and click-and-drag — only recorded if you turn on the app's “Also capture right-clicks, scrolls & drags” setting, which is off by default.

For each one, Proofmark takes a screenshot cropped to just the window you interacted with (or, for a right-click that lands inside a context menu, just the menu itself), notes which application and window title were active (used to write a step description like “Click in Google Chrome”), and timestamps the moment. That's the complete list of what a capture event contains.

03

Screenshots & redaction

Since there's no way to inspect the DOM of an arbitrary desktop application, Proofmark can't know precisely where a password field sits on screen. Instead, it uses a heuristic: before saving a screenshot, it checks the active window's title against a list of terms that tend to signal sensitive content — things like “password,” “sign in,” “account,” “verification code,” “banking,” and “api key.” A match blacks out a region of that screenshot automatically.

This is a best-effort safeguard, not a guarantee. A window with an unrelated title can still contain a sensitive field, and the filter can't read what's actually on screen — only the title bar. Always review a session's screenshots before exporting or sharing a guide, and use the built-in manual redaction/blur tools on anything the automatic filter missed.
04

Where data lives

Locally, in a single file in your Windows user profile — nowhere else. There's no account to create, no sign-in, and no server that Proofmark talks to. The app makes no network requests at all.

Concretely, captured steps and their screenshots are stored as JSON at %AppData%\proofmark-desktop\proofmark-state.json. Standard Windows file permissions restrict that folder to your user account; Proofmark doesn't apply any additional encryption of its own, so if you're capturing especially sensitive workflows on a shared or unencrypted machine, we’d recommend pairing it with full-disk encryption (BitLocker) as you would for any other locally-stored file.

05

Exporting & sharing

When you export a guide — as HTML, PDF, or Word — the file is written only to a location you pick yourself, through a standard save dialog. We never receive a copy, automatically or otherwise; from that point on, the exported file is a regular file on your disk, and sharing it is entirely your call.

06

Your controls

  • Start / Stop Recording — nothing is captured outside an active session.
  • Edit, reorder, or delete any individual step before exporting.
  • Clear Steps — permanently and immediately deletes every captured step from local storage.
  • Manual redaction tools — blur, black-box, or annotate over anything the automatic filter missed, before you export.
07

Third parties

None. Proofmark doesn't include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any other third-party SDK. Nothing you capture is sold, shared, or otherwise made available to anyone — there's simply no channel for it to leave your device through the app itself.

08

Retention & deletion

Captured steps stay in local storage until you clear them yourself, either by deleting individual steps or using Clear Steps. Uninstalling the app removes the program but, like most desktop apps, may leave its local data folder behind — delete %AppData%\proofmark-desktop\ directly if you want to remove it completely.

09

Children's privacy

Proofmark Capture is a general-purpose documentation tool, not directed at children, and it doesn't collect information in a way that identifies a user's age. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.
